Questions to ask before hiring a general contractor in the Bay Area

Draft article · pending content & SEO review.


Choosing a contractor is one of the biggest decisions in any home project. These questions help you compare.

Are you licensed, and can I verify it?

California contractors can be checked on the CSLB website. Parfett Construction's license is #1034776.

How do you communicate during the build?

Ask how often you'll get updates and in what form. We use Buildertrend for daily logs and photos.

Who runs my project day to day?

Know who your point of contact is after the contract is signed.

How do you handle changes?

Look for a clear, written process for change orders and delays.
