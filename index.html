The Parfett approach

We plan in detail so your project runs without surprises, then build it the way we'd want our own home built.

Parfett Construction is a residential general contractor led by Rob Parfett, who has held his general contractor license since 2010. We take on additions, remodels and custom homes for homeowners who care about finish quality and a well-managed build.

Planning comes first: scope, budget, schedule and finishes are settled before construction starts, which limits avoidable change orders. When something does change, we explain why.

  • Integrity
  • Efficiency
  • Clean, safe sites

How a project runs

Six steps, from first call to daily logs.

Every project follows the same sequence, so you always know what happens next and who's responsible.

Start with a scoping call
  1. 01

    Scoping call

    We talk through your goals, budget range, timing and any concepts or drawings you already have.

    Connor
  2. 02

    Site visit

    We walk the property, review existing conditions and confirm what the project will involve.

    Connor
  3. 03

    Plans & engineering

    We review available plans and coordinate the architectural, design and engineering requirements, including permitting.

    Parfett + your design team
  4. 04

    Preliminary budget

    You receive a preliminary budget built from the scope, so decisions are made with real numbers.

    Connor
  5. 05

    Pre-construction handover

    After signing, Connor hands over to Rob or the lead project manager. Together we confirm finishes, budget, schedule and communication.

    Rob / project manager
  6. 06

    Build & communicate

    Construction begins on a clean, safe site. Daily logs and photos are shared through Buildertrend, and Connor checks in monthly.

All FAQs

Are you licensed?
Yes. Parfett Construction Inc. holds California CSLB license #1034776, and Rob Parfett has been a licensed general contractor since 2010. You can verify the license on the CSLB website.
Which areas do you serve?
We're based in Campbell and work across Santa Clara and San Mateo counties, including Los Altos, Mountain View, Menlo Park, Los Gatos and San Jose.
What do I need before contacting you?
Preliminary ideas, concepts or drawings are a great starting point. If you don't have plans yet, we can help coordinate the next planning steps with architects, designers and engineers.
Do you handle permits?
Yes. Permitting coordination is part of how we manage projects, together with architectural, design and engineering collaboration.
How will I know what's happening on site?
We use Buildertrend for daily logs, project photos and client communication, and Connor checks in monthly to keep things on track.
